Teaching

I have taught courses on public law and public policy at various levels and institutions.

At the undergraduate level, my teaching has focused on administrative and constitutional law at law schools. I have also taught specialized courses in public law. At the graduate level, I have taught at the School of Government at the Catholic University of Chile.

Courses

  • School of Government, Pontifical Catholic University of Chile · 2026 · Teaching Assistant

    PGTA for the Master in Public Policy module “Legislation and Regulation”, headed by professor Diego Gil. The courses aims to introduce the students to the dynamics of regulation and public law by covering topics such as the fundamentals of regulation, regulatory institutions, and administrative oversight, among others.

  • Faculty of Law, Adolfo Ibáñez University · 2017 to 2019 · Lecturer

    Lectured at the undergraduate level courses in Constitutional and Administrative law such as Constitutional Justice, Public Procurement Regulation, and Administrative Sanctions.

  • Faculty of Law, University of Chile · 2008 to 2013 · Teaching Assistant

    Assisted professors Carlos Carmona and Raúl Letelier at the undergraduate level in the courses Administrative Law and State Liability.
