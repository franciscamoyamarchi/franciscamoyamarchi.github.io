Bio

Law, regulation, and the conditions for public purpose innovation

I am a lawyer and policy researcher working at the intersections of administrative law, regulation, and public policy, drawing on socio-legal methods, public law theory, and policy analysis.

Education

LLB and Diploma in Economic Law, University of Chile

LLM in Public Law, LSE

My academic journey includes an LLB and a Diploma in Economic Law (University of Chile), and an LLM in Public Law (London School of Economics and Political Science), focused on comparative constitutional and administrative law.

Doctoral work

PhD in Innovation and Public Policy, Institute for Innovation and Public Purpose, UCL

Supervisors: Dr Cecilia Rikap and Professor Jeff King

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Innovation and Public Policy at the Institute for Innovation and Public Purpose, University College London, under the supervision of Dr. Cecilia Rikap and Professor Jeff King. My dissertation examines the role of constitutional and administrative law in shaping the conditions for public purpose innovation.

In government

Chief of the Legal Office, Presidency of Chile

Legal adviser, Presidency of Chile

Law clerk, Constitutional Court of Chile

Before returning to academia, I served as Chief of the Legal Office of the Presidency of Chile, advising on legislative strategy and regulatory design at the highest level of government. Prior roles include legal adviser to the same institution and law clerk at the Constitutional Court of Chile, experiences that enrich my academic work with practical knowledge of law-making and public administration.

You will find out more about my research, teaching, and publications in the other sections of this website: research, teaching, and policy.
